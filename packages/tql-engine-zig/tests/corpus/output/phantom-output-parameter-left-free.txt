title: an output type parameter nothing constrains is left free
grammar: typescript
asserts: types, values

`P`'s derived `Serial` instance needs nothing of `a`, so `Serial (P a)` holds
whatever `a` is, and nothing has to determine it.

--- tql ---
data P a = P deriving (Serial);

main = pure P;
