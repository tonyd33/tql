title: `collect (a <|> b)` collects the union of `a` and `b`
grammar: typescript
asserts: types, values

`collect` gathers whatever the union yields, so a multi-output operand
contributes every one of its values.

--- tql ---
import Data.Function (const);

main = collect $ Filter (const [1, 2]) <|> pure 3;

--- source ---
class A {}

--- values ---
[[1, 2, 3]]

--- types ---
main :: Filter Node [Int]
