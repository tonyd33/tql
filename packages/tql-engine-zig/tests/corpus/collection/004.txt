title: `[a, b]` collects the union of `a` and `b`
grammar: typescript
asserts: types, values

`,` inside brackets is stream union, not an array separator. The bracket
collects whatever the union yields, so a multi-output operand contributes every
one of its values.

--- tql ---
main = [(pure 1, pure 2), pure 3];

--- source ---
class A {}

--- values ---
[[1, 2, 3]]

--- types ---
main : Node -> [[Int]]
