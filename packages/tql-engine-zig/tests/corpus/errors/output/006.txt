title: a function nested in a list is not serializable
grammar: typescript

`[e]` collects `e`'s outputs, so `[pure kind, pure text]` is `[node -> string]`
— a list of functions. `Serial` fails through the list to its element type.

The pre-Q22 program here was `[kind, text]`, which collected a union of two
filters into `[string]` and did not fail at all; `collection/003` is the same
shape and is a passing fixture.

--- tql ---
main = [pure kind, pure text];

--- error ---
category: unsatisfied-constraint
span: 1:8-1:30
`Serial [node -> string]` is not satisfied.
