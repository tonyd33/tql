title: a function nested in a list is not serializable
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`[kind, text]` is `[Node -> String]`, a list of functions. `Serial` fails
through the list to its element type.

`collect (kind <|> text)` looks similar and does not fail at all: it collects
the union of two filters into `[String]`. `collection/003` is the same shape
and is a passing fixture.

--- tql ---
main = pure [kind, text];

--- error ---
category: unsatisfied-constraint
span: 1:1-1:26
`Serial [Node -> String]` is not satisfied.
