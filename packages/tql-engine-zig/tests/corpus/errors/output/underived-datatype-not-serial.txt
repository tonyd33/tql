title: a declared datatype that does not derive `Serial` is not output
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`Maybe` does not derive `Serial`, so it has no JSON encoding.

--- tql ---
data Maybe a = Just a | Nothing;

main = pure (Just 1);

--- error ---
error[unsatisfied-constraint]: `Serial (Maybe Int)` is not satisfied.
