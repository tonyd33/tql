title: a signature whose constraints are not entailed
grammar: typescript

The annotation claims `Ord` for any `a`, but `Ord` is closed over `int` and
`string` and cannot be assumed for a variable.

--- tql ---
smaller : Filter a bool;
smaller = 1 < 2;
main = . | smaller;
