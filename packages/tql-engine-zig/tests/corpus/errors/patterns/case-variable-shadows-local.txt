title: a nested variable in a case alternative may not shadow a local
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

--- tql ---
import Data.Function (const);

f x xs = case xs of {
  [x] -> x;
  _ -> 0;
};

main = const [f 1 [2]];

--- error ---
error[shadowed-local]: `x` in a pattern would shadow the local `x`; bind another name and compare after the bind
 --> 4:4
  |
4 |   [x] -> x;
  |    ^
