title: a member of a recursive group whose scheme is ambiguous
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`g` calls `f` at `Maybe e` for an `e` nothing fixes, so its scheme would be
`Eq e => Int -> Bool`. It is rejected where it is defined, though `main` only
uses `f`.

--- tql ---
f x = if x == x then True else g 1;
g y = f (head []);
main = pure (f (Just 1));

--- error ---
error[ambiguous-constraint]: `Eq a` is ambiguous: nothing determines its type.
 --> 2:1
  |
2 | g y = f (head []);
  | ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
