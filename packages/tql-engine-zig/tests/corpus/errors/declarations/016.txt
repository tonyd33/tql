title: a user definition may not collide with a prelude symbol
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`select` is an ordinary prelude definition, but it is still a global symbol and
may not be redefined at top level. A *local* binding of the same name is fine;
see PRE-17.

--- tql ---
select p = p;
main = 1;

--- error ---
category: symbol-collision
span: 1:1-1:14
The colliding definition.
