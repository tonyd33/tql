title: a user definition may not collide with a prelude symbol
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`keep` is an ordinary prelude definition, but it is still a global symbol and
may not be redefined at top level. A *local* binding of the same name is fine;
see PRE-17.

--- tql ---
keep p = p;
main = 1;

--- error ---
error[symbol-collision]: `keep` collides with an existing symbol
 --> 1:1
  |
1 | keep p = p;
  | ^^^^^^^^^^^
