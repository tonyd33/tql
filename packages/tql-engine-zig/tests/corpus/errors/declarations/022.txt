title: a signature may not drop a `Sized` constraint
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`length` needs `Sized a`, and `Node` is not `Sized`. Accepting the signature
would let `size c` at `Node` through.

--- tql ---
size :: a -> Int;
size x = length x;
main = children | arr (\c -> size c);

--- error ---
error[signature-mismatch]: `Sized a` is not satisfied.
 --> 1:1
  |
1 | size :: a -> Int;
  | ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
