title: a derived instance over the last parameter carries its context
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`Functor (Wrap f)` holds only under `Functor f`, so a signature without it
does not give what `map` at `Wrap f` needs.

--- tql ---
data Wrap f a = Wrap (f a) deriving (Functor);

again :: Wrap f Int -> Wrap f Int;
again w = map (* 2) w;

main = pure 1;

--- error ---
error[signature-mismatch]: `Functor f` is needed, but the signature of `again` does not give it.
 --> 4:11
  |
4 | again w = map (* 2) w;
  |           ^^^
