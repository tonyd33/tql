title: a signature may not annotate a wired symbol
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

Wired symbols are supplied by the implementation and need no source
declaration.

--- tql ---
kind : Node -> String;
main = arr kind;

--- error ---
error[symbol-collision]: `kind` collides with an existing symbol
 --> 1:1
  |
1 | kind : Node -> String;
  | ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
