title: a constructor field may not leave out a type's arguments
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`List` takes one argument. A bare `List` is not a type a field can hold.

--- tql ---
type T = C List;
main = pure 1;

--- error ---
category: type-mismatch
span: 1:12-1:16
`List` takes 1 type argument(s), given 0
