title: a lambda naming two parameters alike is an error
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

A nested lambda may still shadow: `\x -> \x -> x` is accepted.

--- tql ---
f = \x x -> x;
main = pure (f 1 2);

--- error ---
error[duplicate-definition]: `x` names two parameters
 --> 1:8
  |
1 | f = \x x -> x;
  |        ^
