title: a regex that makes `\d` Unicode is a compile error
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`\d`, `\w` and `\s` match ASCII only. `(*UCP)` would widen them.

--- tql ---
main = pure $ "٧" ~ r"(*UCP)^\d$";

--- error ---
error[invalid-regex]: `(*UCP)^\d$` uses `(*UCP)`, which tql regexes do not support
