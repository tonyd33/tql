title: a constructor field may not be a Range
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`Range` names a record type, and constructor fields are not records yet.

--- tql ---
data Span = Span Range;
main = const [];

--- error ---
error[type-mismatch]: a constructor field may not be a record yet
 --> 1:18
  |
1 | data Span = Span Range;
  |                  ^^^^^
