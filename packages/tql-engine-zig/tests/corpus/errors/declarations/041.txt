title: a derived class must hold inside a field's type arguments
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

Lists are ordered when their elements are, and nodes are not.

--- tql ---
type T = T [Node] deriving (Ord);
main = pure 1;

--- error ---
category: unsatisfied-constraint
span: 1:12-1:18
`Ord Node` is not satisfied, so `T` cannot derive `Ord`
