title: a signature may not drop a constraint the body raises
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

The body compares its arguments, so it needs `Eq a`. A signature cannot write
a constraint, so `a -> a -> Bool` claims more than the body supports, even
though every use here is at `Int`.

--- tql ---
same : a -> a -> Bool;
same x y = x = y;
main = pure (same 1 1);

--- error ---
category: signature-mismatch
span: 1:1-1:23
`Eq a` is not satisfied.
