title: a signature may not drop a constraint the body raises
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

The body compares its arguments, so it needs `Eq a`. The signature does not
write it, so `a -> a -> Bool` claims more than the body supports, even though
every use here is at `Int`.

--- tql ---
same :: a -> a -> Bool;
same x y = x == y;
main = pure (same 1 1);

--- error ---
error[signature-mismatch]: `Eq a` is needed, but the signature of `same` does not give it.
 --> 2:12
  |
2 | same x y = x == y;
  |            ^^^^^^
