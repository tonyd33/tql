title: a signature's variables may not be identified with each other
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

Both branches of the `if` share one type, so the body forces `a` and `b` to be
the same. The signature claims they are independent.

--- tql ---
k : a -> b -> a;
k x y = if true then x else y;
main = pure (k 1 2);

--- error ---
category: signature-mismatch
span: 1:1-1:17
Expected `a -> b -> a`, found `a -> a -> a`.
