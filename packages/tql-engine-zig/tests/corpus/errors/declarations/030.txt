title: `Eq` does not cover `Ord`
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`<` needs `Ord a`. There are no superclasses, so neither class covers the
other.

--- tql ---
less : Eq a => a -> a -> Bool;
less x y = x < y;
main = const [less 1 2];

--- error ---
error[signature-mismatch]: `Ord a` is not satisfied.
 --> 1:1
  |
1 | less : Eq a => a -> a -> Bool;
  | ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
