title: a kind from a different grammar is unknown
grammar: typescript

`function_definition` is a C kind. Under the typescript grammar the equivalent
is `function_declaration`, so this must be rejected rather than silently
yielding nothing.

--- tql ---
main = . // :function_definition;

--- error ---
category: unknown-kind
span: 1:13-1:33
The `:function_definition` selector.
