title: an unknown static field is a compile error
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

A field unknown to the grammar is rejected. A field the grammar knows but a
particular node lacks yields nothing instead.

--- tql ---
main = children | of_kind :class_declaration | #nonexistent_field;

--- error ---
error[unknown-field]: `nonexistent_field` is not a field in this grammar
