title: a member of a recursive group whose `Serial` context is ambiguous
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`g` calls `f` at `Maybe e` for an `e` nothing fixes, so its scheme would be
`Serial e => Int -> Bool`. It is rejected where it is defined, as the same
group under `Eq` is.

--- tql ---
s :: Serial a => a -> Bool;
s x = True;

f x = if s x then True else g 1;
g y = f (head []);

main = pure (f (Just 1));

--- error ---
error[ambiguous-constraint]: `Serial a` is ambiguous: nothing determines its type.

  |
5 | g y = f (head []);
