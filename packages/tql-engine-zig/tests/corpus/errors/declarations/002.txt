title: a `let`-bound name is not visible outside its group
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`inner` is scoped to the `let` body. The reference after `in` closes is
unresolved.

--- tql ---
main =
  let {
    inner = 1
  } in
    inner;
outside = inner;

--- error ---
category: unresolved-name
span: 6:11-6:16
`inner` in `outside`.
