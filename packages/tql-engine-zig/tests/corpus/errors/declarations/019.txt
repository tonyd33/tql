title: `main` may not have a function type
grammar: typescript

The output of a query is not a function. Two parameters on the head is one
spelling of that error, and `main a = \b -> e` and `main = \a -> \b -> e` are
the same function written differently — so the rejection is of the type, not of
where the parameters were written.

--- tql ---
main root extra = root;
