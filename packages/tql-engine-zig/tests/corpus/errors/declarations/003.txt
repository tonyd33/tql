title: a `do` binding is not visible after its block
grammar: typescript

The binding is branch-local to the block.

--- tql ---
main root =
  do {
    c <- (children | is_kind :class_declaration) root;
    (.name | text) c
  };
after = c;

--- error ---
category: unresolved-name
span: 6:9-6:10
`c` in `after`.
