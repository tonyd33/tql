title: a constructor field may not leave out a type's arguments
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`List` takes one argument. A bare `List` is not a type a field can hold.

--- tql ---
data T = C List;
main = always 1;

--- error ---
error[kind-mismatch]: `List` has kind `Type -> Type`, but kind `Type` is expected
 --> 1:12
  |
1 | data T = C List;
  |            ^^^^
