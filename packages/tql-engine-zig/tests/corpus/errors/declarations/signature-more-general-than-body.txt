title: a signature more general than its body is rejected
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

The body infers `Node -> Kind`, which is less general than the signature
`a -> a`.

--- tql ---
f :: a -> a;
f = kind;
main = arr f;

--- error ---
error[signature-mismatch]: Expected `Node`, found `Kind`.
 --> 1:1
  |
1 | f :: a -> a;
  | ^^^^^^^^^^^^
