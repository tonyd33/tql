title: dynamic kind tests are not part of 0.3
grammar: typescript

A `:kind` selector is static syntax. A computed string cannot stand in for one.

--- tql ---
main =
  let {
    k = "class_declaration"
  } in
    . / :k;

--- error ---
category: unknown-kind
span: 5:9-5:11
The `:k` selector. `k` is not a grammar kind, and the selector does not
evaluate its operand.
