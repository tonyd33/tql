title: dynamic kind tests are not part of 0.3
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

A `:kind` selector is static syntax. A computed string cannot stand in for one.

`k` is not a grammar kind, and the selector does not evaluate its operand.

--- tql ---
main =
  let { k = "class_declaration"; }
  in children | of_kind :k;

--- error ---
error[unknown-kind]: `k` is not a node kind in this grammar
 --> 3:25
  |
3 |   in children | of_kind :k;
  |                         ^^
