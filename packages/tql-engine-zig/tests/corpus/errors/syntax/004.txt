title: a regex-match chain does not type
grammar: typescript

Well-formed syntax, rejected by typing, as in E-ASSOC-01. Parses as
`("a" ~ r"a") ~ r"b"`, so the left operand of the second `~` is a `bool`
where `~` requires a `string`.

--- tql ---
main = "a" ~ r"a" ~ r"b";

--- error ---
category: type-mismatch
span: 1:8-1:25
The whole chain.
