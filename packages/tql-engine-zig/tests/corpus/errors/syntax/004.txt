title: a regex-match chain does not type
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

Well-formed syntax, rejected by typing. Parses as
`("a" ~ r"a") ~ r"b"`, so the left operand of the second `~` is a `bool`
where `~` requires a `string`.

--- tql ---
main = "a" ~ r"a" ~ r"b";

--- error ---
error[type-mismatch]: Expected `String`, found `Bool`.
 --> 1:8
  |
1 | main = "a" ~ r"a" ~ r"b";
  |        ^^^^^^^^^^
