title: an unclosed `let` group
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`in` arrives where the binding group's `}` was required.

Recovery discards the whole declaration rather than resynchronizing inside it,
so the span covers the line.

--- tql ---
main = let { x = 1 in x;

--- error ---
error[parse]: syntax error
 --> 1:1
  |
1 | main = let { x = 1 in x;
  | ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
