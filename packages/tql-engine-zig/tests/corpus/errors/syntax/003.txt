title: a comparison chain does not type
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`a < b < c` must be grouped. The chain is well-formed syntax: it parses as
`(1 < 2) < 3`, and the rejection is a typing one. The outer `<` compares a
`Bool` with an `Int`.

A better diagnostic than the bare mismatch is owed here: the checker can see
that the operand of a comparison is itself a comparison, and saying the
operators do not chain is more useful than naming the types.

--- tql ---
main = 1 < 2 < 3;

--- error ---
category: type-mismatch
span: 1:16-1:17
The `3`.
