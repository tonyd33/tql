title: a comparison chain does not type
grammar: typescript

`a < b < c` must be grouped. The chain is well-formed syntax: it parses as
`(1 < 2) < 3`, and the rejection is a typing one — the outer `<` requires
`Ord` and its left operand is a `bool`, which has no instance.

Stage 3 owes a better diagnostic than the bare `Ord bool` constraint. The
checker can see that the operand of a comparison is itself a comparison, and
saying the operators do not chain is more useful than naming the instance.

--- tql ---
main = 1 < 2 < 3;

--- error ---
category: type-mismatch
span: 1:8-1:17
The whole chain.
