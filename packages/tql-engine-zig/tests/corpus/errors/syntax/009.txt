title: `<|>` is not the union operator
grammar: typescript

Union is `,`. `<|>` is a legacy spelling from `work/real_world/TQL.md`.

--- tql ---
main = 1 <|> 2;

--- error ---
category: parse
span: any
Removed syntax. `<` lexes as a comparison operator, so the error starts at
`|>`; recovering the whole token would mean teaching the grammar a spelling
that no longer exists.
