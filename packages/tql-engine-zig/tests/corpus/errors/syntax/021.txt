title: a literal pattern is a syntax error at the literal
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

Patterns are constructors, variables and `_`.

--- tql ---
f n = case n of { 3 -> 1; _ -> 0; };

main = pure (f 3);

--- error ---
category: parse
span: 1:19-1:26
From the `3` to the end of its alternative.
