title: `@` variables are removed
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`@name` is legacy 0.2 variable syntax. `@` is not a token in 0.3, so the line
fails at the `@` itself.

--- tql ---
main = children | is_kind :class_declaration | @name;

--- error ---
category: parse
span: any
Removed syntax. A generic parse error is enough; the exact span is not
normative, because pinning it would mean teaching the grammar the shape of
`@name` in order to report it as one token.
