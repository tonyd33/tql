title: a literal parameter is a syntax error at the literal
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

Parameters are names. Patterns are written in `case` alternatives and on the
left of `<-`.

--- tql ---
f 3 = 1;

main = always (f 3);

--- error ---
error[parse]: syntax error
 --> 1:3
  |
1 | f 3 = 1;
  |   ^
