title: `as` binding is removed
grammar: typescript

Replaced by `do` stream binding. `as` is not a keyword, so this parses as an
application of three operands and fails when `as` does not resolve, exactly as
`def` does in E-LEGACY-01.

Three names are unresolved, not one. `as` groups as `(:class_declaration as) c`,
so the `c` it was meant to bind is an ordinary operand rather than a binder, and
the `c` in `c.name` has nothing to resolve against either.

--- tql ---
main = . / :class_declaration as c | c.name | text;
