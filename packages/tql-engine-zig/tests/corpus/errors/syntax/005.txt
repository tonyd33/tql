title: a `do` block with no final expression
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

Every `do` block has exactly one final expression. This block ends after a
statement, so the `}` arrives where the final expression was required.

--- tql ---
main root =
  do {
    c <- (children | is_kind :class_declaration) root;
  };

--- error ---
category: parse
span: any
Rejection is what matters. Whether the diagnostic points at the `}` or at the
block it closes is a presentation choice, and pinning it here would freeze a
recovery detail the language does not fix.
