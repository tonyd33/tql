title: a declaration inside a right-hand side
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

Declarations are top-level only. Inside a `do` block, `helper = 1` is not a
binding. `<-` binds and `=` is equality, so it reads as an expression
statement comparing an undefined name.

--- tql ---
main =
  do {
    helper = 1;
    .
  };

--- error ---
category: unresolved-name
span: 3:5-3:11
