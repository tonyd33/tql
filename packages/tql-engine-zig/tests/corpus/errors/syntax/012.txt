title: there is no optional-binding form
grammar: typescript

`@var?` optional binding is legacy 0.2 syntax with no 0.3 analogue.

--- tql ---
main = . / :class_declaration | @name?;

--- error ---
category: parse
span: any
Removed syntax. The `@` and the `?` are separate errors either side of an
intact `name`, so no single span covers the sequence.
