title: `Control.Arrow`'s `arr` beside the prelude's filter `arr` is ambiguous
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

The prelude keeps the filter `arr` until `Filter` is an `Arrow`.

--- tql ---
import Control.Arrow (Arrow(..));

main = children | arr text;

--- error ---
