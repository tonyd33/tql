title: records have no order
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

A structural record's only field order is alphabetical, so no record has `Ord`. Compare through a field.

--- tql ---
main root = [range root < range root];

--- error ---
error[unsatisfied-constraint]: `Ord Range` is not satisfied.
