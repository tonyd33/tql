title: a failed binding leaves errors about what it uses reported
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`h` fails through `g`, and its own `Applicative` constraint, which no use of
`h` can fix, is not reported. `one`'s type is not `h`'s to poison, so the
errors that follow from `one 3` are reported.

--- tql ---
g y = if y then 1 else "a";
one = pure 1;
h = (one, pure (g True));
other = seq h (one 3);
main = Kleisli $ \_ -> one;

--- source ---
f(x);

--- error ---
error[type-mismatch]: Expected `Int`, found `String`.
 --> 1:24
  |
1 | g y = if y then 1 else "a";
  |                        ^^^

error[unsatisfied-constraint]: `Applicative ((->) Int)` is not satisfied.
 --> 2:7
  |
2 | one = pure 1;
  |       ^^^^

error[type-mismatch]: Expected `List`, found `(->) Int`.
 --> 5:1
  |
5 | main = Kleisli $ \_ -> one;
  | ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
