title: a body that disagrees with its signature is reported at the subterm
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

The body is checked against `Int -> Int`, so `return_string z` is checked
against `Int`, and the mismatch is reported there, not at the signature
(Static semantics: Bindings, T-Sig).

--- tql ---
f :: Int -> Int;
f x = do {
  let y = x + 1;
  let z = y + 2;
  return_string z;
};
return_string z = "s";
main = pure (f 1);

--- source ---
f(x);

--- error ---
error[type-mismatch]: Expected `Int`, found `String`.
 --> 5:3
  |
5 |   return_string z;
  |   ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
