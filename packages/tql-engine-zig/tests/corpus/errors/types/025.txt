title: `not` applied to a comparison's left operand is a type error
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`not 1 = 1` groups as `(not 1) = 1`, and `not` takes a `Bool`.

--- tql ---
main = pure $ not 1 = 1;

--- error ---
error[type-mismatch]: Expected `Bool`, found `Int`.
 --> 1:19
  |
1 | main = pure $ not 1 = 1;
  |                   ^
