title: every member of a recursive group carries the group's constraints
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`f` raises `Eq a` and `g` shares its type through the recursion, so `g` must
carry `Eq a` too. Calling `g` at `Regex` is refused just as calling `f` is.

--- tql ---
f x = if x = x then 1 else g x;
g x = f x;
main = always (g r"a");

--- error ---
error[unsatisfied-constraint]: `Eq Regex` is not satisfied.
 --> 3:16
  |
3 | main = always (g r"a");
  |                ^
