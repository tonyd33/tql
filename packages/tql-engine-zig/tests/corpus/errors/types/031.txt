title: `>>` does not sequence filters
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`>>` sequences lists, as a `do` block does. A filter is a function.

--- tql ---
main = children >> descendants;

--- error ---
error[type-mismatch]: Expected `[a]`, found `Node -> [Node]`.
 --> 1:8
  |
1 | main = children >> descendants;
  |        ^^^^^^^^
