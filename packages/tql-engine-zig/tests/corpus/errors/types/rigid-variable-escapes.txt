title: a rigid variable cannot escape its signature
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`g`'s signature says it returns any `b`, but its body returns `x`, whose
type is fixed outside `g` (Static semantics: Judgements).

--- tql ---
f x = let {
  g :: b -> b;
  g y = x;
} in g 1;
main = pure (f 0);

--- source ---
f(x);

--- error ---
error[type-mismatch]: Expected `b`, found `a`. `b` is rigid, bound by the signature of `g`.
 --> 3:9
  |
3 |   g y = x;
  |         ^
