title: `>>` does not sequence filters
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

`>>` sequences a monad's values, as a `do` block does. A filter is a
function, and functions are not a monad.

--- tql ---
main = children >> descendants;

--- error ---
error[unsatisfied-constraint]: `Monad ((->) Node)` is not satisfied.
 --> 1:8
  |
1 | main = children >> descendants;
  |        ^^^^^^^^
