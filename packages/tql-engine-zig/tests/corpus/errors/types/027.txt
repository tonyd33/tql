title: a nested pattern that leaves a shape out names it
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

A one-element list reaches no alternative.

--- tql ---
f xs = case xs of { Cons a (Cons b Nil) -> [a]; Nil -> []; };

main = pure (f [1, 2]);

--- error ---
category: type-mismatch
span: 1:8-1:61
The whole `case`, reported as `Cons _ Nil` is not matched.
