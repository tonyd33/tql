title: equality on two empty lists is ambiguous
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

Nothing fixes the element type, so no instance of `Eq` can be chosen.

--- tql ---
main = pure ([] == []);

--- error ---
error[ambiguous-constraint]: `Eq [a]` is ambiguous: nothing determines its type.
