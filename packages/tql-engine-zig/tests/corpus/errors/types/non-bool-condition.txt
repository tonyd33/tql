title: a non-boolean condition is a compile error
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

There is no truthiness. A condition must have type `Bool`.

--- tql ---
main = pure (if 1 then "yes" else "no");

--- error ---
error[type-mismatch]: Expected `Bool`, found `Int`.
 --> 1:17
  |
1 | main = pure (if 1 then "yes" else "no");
  |                 ^
