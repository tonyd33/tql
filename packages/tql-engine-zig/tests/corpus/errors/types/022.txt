title: tuple types do not exist
grammar: typescript
asserts: error

There is no tuple type or tuple literal. `,` is only a separator, so a
parenthesized comma expression does not parse.

The stray `,` never constructs a pair.

--- tql ---
main = (1, "a");

--- error ---
error[parse]: syntax error
 --> 1:10
  |
1 | main = (1, "a");
  |          ^
