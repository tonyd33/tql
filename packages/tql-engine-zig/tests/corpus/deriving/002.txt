title: a derived Ord orders by constructor declaration order first
grammar: typescript

`Circle` is declared before `Square`, so every circle is less than every
square whatever their fields.

--- tql ---
type Shape = Circle Int | Square Int deriving (Eq, Ord);
main = const [Circle 9 < Square 1, Square 1 > Circle 9, Circle 1 < Circle 2, Square 2 <= Square 2];
