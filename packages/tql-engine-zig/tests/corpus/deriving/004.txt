title: a derived class on a parameterized type holds at its arguments
grammar: typescript
asserts: types, values

`Nothing` is declared after `Just`, so it is the greater.

--- tql ---
type Maybe a = Just a | Nothing deriving (Eq, Ord);
main = const [Just 1 < Just 2, Just 5 < Nothing, Just (Just 1) = Just (Just 1), Just Nothing > Just (Just 9)];

--- source ---
class A {}

--- values ---
[true,true,true,true]

--- types ---
main : Node -> [Bool]
