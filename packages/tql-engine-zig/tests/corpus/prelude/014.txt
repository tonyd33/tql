title: `or_else` yields the primary when it has results
grammar: typescript
asserts: types, values

`or_else` runs `primary` once and yields its results, or `fallback`'s when it
has none.

--- tql ---
main =
  children | of_kind :class_declaration
  | or_else (#name | arr text) (pure "anonymous");

--- source ---
class A {}

--- values ---
["A"]

--- types ---
main :: Node -> [String]
