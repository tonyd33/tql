title: a function in a `let` group is polymorphic to the other bindings
grammar: typescript
asserts: core, types, values

`me` refers to nothing else in the group, so it is generalized before `a` and
`b` are checked, and they use it at `Int` and at `String`.

--- tql ---
main =
  let {
    me x = x;
    a = me 1;
    b = me "s";
  }
  in pure (a + length b);
