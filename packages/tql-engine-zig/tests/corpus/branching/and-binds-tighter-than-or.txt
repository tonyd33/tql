title: `and` binds tighter than `or`
grammar: typescript
asserts: core, types, values

`true or true and false` groups as `true or (true and false)`. Grouping left
to right would yield `false`.

--- tql ---
main = pure $ true or true and false;

--- source ---
class A {}

--- values ---
[true]

--- types ---
main :: Filter Node Bool

--- core ---
main = pure (or True (and True False))
