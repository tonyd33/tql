title: `not p and q` groups as `(not p) and q`
grammar: typescript
asserts: types, values

`and` binds looser than `not`. If it grouped as `not (p and q)`, the result
would be `[true]`.

--- tql ---
main = pure $ not false and false;

--- source ---
class A {}

--- values ---
[false]

--- types ---
main :: Node -> [Bool]
