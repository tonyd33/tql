title: `not a = b` groups as `not (a = b)`
grammar: typescript
asserts: types, values

`not` binds looser than comparison, so its operand extends rightward through
the comparison level. The rejected reading `(not 1) = 1` does not typecheck,
since `not` requires `Bool`.

--- tql ---
main = pure (not 1 = 1);

--- source ---
class A {}

--- values ---
[false]

--- types ---
main : Node -> [Bool]
