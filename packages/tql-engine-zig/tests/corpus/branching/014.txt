title: `and` and `or` are left-associative
grammar: typescript
asserts: core, types, values

`false and true or true` groups as `(false and true) or true`. The alternative
grouping `false and (true or true)` would yield `false`.

--- tql ---
main = pure $ false and true or true;

--- source ---
class A {}

--- values ---
[true]

--- types ---
main :: Node -> [Bool]

--- core ---
main = pure (or (and False True) True)
