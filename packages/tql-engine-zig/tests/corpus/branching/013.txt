title: application still binds tighter than `not`
grammar: typescript

`not is_a "A"` is `not (is_a "A")`. Application binds tighter than `not`, so the
looser grouping of `not` over comparison does not reach it. The rejected reading
`(not is_a) "A"` applies `not` to a function and does not typecheck.
