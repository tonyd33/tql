title: `not` binds looser than comparison but tighter than `and`
grammar: typescript
asserts: types, values

`not 1 = 2 and false` groups as `(not (1 = 2)) and false`. The `not` swallows
the comparison to its right and stops before `and`.

--- tql ---
main = pure (not 1 = 2 and false);

--- source ---
class A {}

--- values ---
[false]
