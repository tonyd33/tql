title: `not` over a comparison takes `$`
grammar: typescript
asserts: core, types, values

`not` is an ordinary function, so `not 1 = 1` would apply it to `1`. `$` hands
it the whole comparison.

--- tql ---
main = always $ not $ 1 = 1;

--- source ---
class A {}

--- values ---
[false]

--- types ---
main :: Node -> [Bool]

--- core ---
main = always (not (eq 1 1))
