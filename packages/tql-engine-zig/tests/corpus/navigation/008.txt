title: `named_children` keeps every named child
grammar: typescript
asserts: types, values

`class_declaration` has two named children, `name` and `body`. Anonymous tokens
such as `class`, `{`, and `}` are not visited.

--- tql ---
main = children | of_kind :class_declaration | named_children | arr kind;

--- source ---
class A {}

--- values ---
["type_identifier", "class_body"]

--- types ---
main :: Node -> [String]
