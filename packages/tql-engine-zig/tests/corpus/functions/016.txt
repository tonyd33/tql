grammar: typescript

`pick` is generalized after its SCC is checked, so it may be instantiated at
`int` and at `string` in the same program.

--- tql ---
pick x y = x;
main = pure (pick 1 2), (pure (pick "ab" "cd") | length);

--- source ---
class A {}

--- values ---
[1, 2]

--- types ---
pick : a -> b -> a
main : Node -> [Int]
