title: a filter returned from a function

`matcher` constructs a filter from a string. Ordinary application builds the
filter; the pipe executes it.

--- tql ---
matcher expected = \c -> [text c.name = expected];
main = children | is_kind :class_declaration | matcher "A";

--- source ---
