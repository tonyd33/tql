title: modulo by zero is undefined
grammar: typescript
asserts: types

Division and modulo by zero have no defined result. The rule that they yield
an empty stream was written when operators were filters and could produce no
output; a scalar operator has nowhere to put the absence. A `Maybe` is what
gives this an answer, and the language does not have one yet.

--- tql ---
main = pure $ 1 % 0;

--- source ---
class A {}

--- types ---
main :: Node -> [Int]
