title: product operators enumerate left operand outermost
grammar: typescript
pending: values

The rule under test is ordering. Left-major means the left operand is the outer
generator: 10 pairs with 1 then 2, then 20 pairs with 1 then 2, giving
11, 12, 21, 22. A right-major enumeration would vary the left operand fastest
and give 11, 21, 12, 22.

--- tql ---
main = (10, 20) + (1, 2);

--- source ---
class A {}

--- values ---
[11, 12, 21, 22]
