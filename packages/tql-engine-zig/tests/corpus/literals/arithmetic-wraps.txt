title: addition, subtraction and multiplication wrap
grammar: typescript
asserts: values

`Int` is 64-bit two's complement in every build mode.

--- tql ---
largest = 9223372036854775807;

least = 0 - largest - 1;

main = pure [
  largest + 1 == least,
  0 - largest - 2 == largest,
  4611686018427387904 * 2 == least
];
