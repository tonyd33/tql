title: the closing quote ends the literal
grammar: typescript
pending: values

`r"^A"` is a complete regex literal; the following `,` begins a union, not more
pattern. Delimiting with `"` rather than `/` is what leaves `/` with exactly
one meaning in every position (OPEN-QUESTIONS Q9).

--- tql ---
main = "A" ~ r"^A", "B" ~ r"^A";

--- source ---
class A {}
