title: a partial operation prunes only the offending pair
grammar: typescript
asserts: types
pending: values

The right operand yields three moduli; only the zero pair is dropped, and the
surviving pairs keep their left-major order.

--- tql ---
main = \root -> do { d <- (pure 5, pure 0, pure 7) root; pure (12 % d) root };

--- source ---
class A {}

--- values ---
[2, 5]

--- types ---
