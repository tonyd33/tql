title: a regex that backtracks exponentially still matches
grammar: typescript
asserts: values

`(a|aa)+b` splits a run of `a`s in exponentially many ways before the `c`
fails it. The matcher follows every alternative at once instead, so the
second alternative is found and `!~` is its negation.

--- tql ---
s = "aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaac";
main = pure [s ~ r"^(?:(a|aa)+b|a+c)$", s !~ r"^(?:(a|aa)+b|a+c)$", "aac" ~ r"^(?:(a|aa)+b|a+c)$"];
