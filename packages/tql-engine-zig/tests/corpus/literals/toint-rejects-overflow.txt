title: `toint` gives `Nothing` for a value that does not fit `Int`
grammar: typescript
asserts: types, values

`9223372036854775807` is the largest `Int`; one more does not fit.

--- tql ---
import Data.Int (toint);

main = pure [
  toint "9223372036854775807" == Just 9223372036854775807,
  toint "9223372036854775808" == Nothing
];
