title: a dictionary a recursive call passes through is substituted
grammar: typescript

`total`'s recursive call passes its own dictionary, and the one outside
call passes `Sized List`.

--- tql ---
total :: Sized s => [s] -> Int;
total xss = case xss of {
  Nil -> 0;
  Cons h t -> length h + total t;
};

main = Filter (\_ -> [total [[1], [2, 3]]]);
