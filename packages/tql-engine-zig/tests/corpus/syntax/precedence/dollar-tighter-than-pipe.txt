title: dollar application binds tighter than pipe
grammar: typescript
asserts: tql_tree

`|` is the loosest operator, so a stage takes its argument with `$`.

--- tql ---
main a f p = a | f $ p | f $ p;

--- source ---

--- tql tree ---
(source_file (define main (params a f p) (| (| a (apply f p)) (apply f p))))
