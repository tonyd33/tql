title: a tight infix minus subtracts
grammar: typescript
asserts: tql_tree, values

Only a prefix `-` starts a negative literal, as under GHC's `LexicalNegation`.
`x-1` is `x - 1`, not `x` applied to `-1`.

--- tql ---
x = 5;
main = Filter (\_ -> [x-1, 5-1]);

--- source ---
class A {}

--- tql tree ---
(source_file (define x (params) 5) (define main (params) (apply Filter (paren (lambda (params _) (list (- x 1) (- 5 1)))))))

--- values ---
[4,4]
