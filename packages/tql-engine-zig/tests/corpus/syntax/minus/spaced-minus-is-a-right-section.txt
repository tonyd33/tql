title: a spaced minus sections like any operator
grammar: typescript
asserts: tql_tree, values

Negative literals are decided by the lexer, so a loose `-` is the
subtraction operator and `(- 1)` is its right section.

--- tql ---
main = Filter (\_ -> map (- 1) [5]);

--- source ---
class A {}

--- tql tree ---
(source_file (define main (params) (apply Filter (paren (lambda (params _) (apply (apply map (section - _ 1)) (list 5)))))))

--- values ---
[4]
