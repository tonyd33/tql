title: a datatype's row parameter lacks the labels its fields need it to lack
grammar: typescript
asserts: values

`r` lacks `x` in `W`'s declaration, so it lacks `x` in `h`'s signature,
and through `V`, whose field is `W r` (Types: Records and rows).

--- tql ---
data W r = W {x: Int | r};
data V r = V (W r);

h :: W r -> Int;
h w = case w of {
  W v -> v.x;
};

k :: V r -> Int;
k v = case v of {
  V w -> h w;
};

main = Kleisli $ \_ -> [h $ W {x = 1, y = 2}, k $ V $ W {x = 3}];

--- source ---
class A {}

--- values ---
[1,3]
