title: `zip` pairs two lists to the shorter, and `unzip` is lazy in its spine
grammar: typescript
asserts: types, values

`unzip` takes the first two of an infinite list of pairs.

--- tql ---
import Data.List (zip, unzip, take);
import Data.Tuple (fst);

nats n = Cons n (nats (n + 1));

main = pure (zip [1, 2, 3] ["a", "b"], unzip [(1, "a"), (2, "b")], take 2 (fst (unzip (zip (nats 0) (nats 10)))));
